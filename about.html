About Modola Mining

A Nigerian-owned, compliant, cadastre-registered operator building a phased, de-risked pipeline of lithium and gold assets.

Company Overview

Exploring, acquiring and responsibly operating mineral assets

Modola Mining Company Limited is a Nigerian solid minerals company headquartered in Lagos, established to explore, develop and responsibly operate mineral assets across Nigeria's most prospective geological terrains. The company was founded to participate in Nigeria's accelerating minerals renaissance, with an initial strategic focus on lithium and gold, and a broader interest in the associated pegmatite and precious-metal minerals that typically occur alongside them, including tantalite, columbite, cassiterite and gemstone by-products.

Our approach is deliberately phased and risk-managed. We are building Modola from a Lagos base outward: beginning with exploration and small-scale operations in the South-West of Nigeria, and progressing over the medium to long term toward mining leases in the Middle Belt and selected, relatively stable Northern states — the regions that host Nigeria's most significant lithium pegmatite fields and gold belts. Site selection at every stage is guided as much by geology as by security, community relations, and access to infrastructure.

Nigeria's Ministry of Solid Minerals Development has, in recent years, confirmed major lithium and gold occurrences across the South-West and Middle Belt, and the Federal Government has actively courted investment into local value-addition rather than raw ore export. Modola is positioning itself to be a credible, compliant, Nigerian-owned participant in that value chain — from licensed exploration through to production and supply into domestic and export off-take markets.

Regulatory Standing & Compliance

Duly incorporated, fully compliant

Modola Mining Company Limited is duly incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act. All exploration and mining activity is conducted, or planned to be conducted, strictly within the framework of the Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act 2007 and its regulations, under the oversight of the Federal Ministry of Solid Minerals Development and the Mining Cadastre Office (MCO).

Holding valid mineral titles (Exploration License, Small-Scale Mining Lease or Mining Lease, as applicable) for every site of operation, obtained through the MCO's cadastral registry.

Submission of geoscientific data, exploration records and ore-reserve reports to the Nigerian Geological Survey Agency (NGSA) and MCO as required by law.

Environmental Impact Assessment and Mines Environmental Compliance Department clearance ahead of any mechanized operation.

Adherence to the Federal Government's local value-addition policy on lithium, which restricts export of raw, unprocessed ore.

Formal engagement with host community structures, traditional institutions and, where applicable, artisanal mining cooperatives operating within our license areas.

Corporate & Organizational Structure

Governance built to scale

Modola is governed by a Board of Directors, with day-to-day management vested in the Managing Director/CEO and a leadership team spanning operations, finance, exploration, HSE and community relations, legal/compliance, and commercial functions. The structure is deliberately built to scale — additional site-level operations managers are added as each new region comes online.
