What We Do

Modola Mining is focused on three complementary product lines, reflecting the mineral associations most common in Nigeria's South-West and Middle Belt pegmatite and schist belt provinces.

Product Line 01

Lithium

Hard-rock spodumene and lepidolite ore, targeting the high-grade pegmatite fields of the South-West (Oyo, Ekiti, Kwara) and the Middle Belt "Lithium Hub" states of Nasarawa and Kogi, in line with the Federal Government's local value-addition policy, which requires beneficiation rather than raw-ore export.

Product Line 02

Gold

Alluvial and primary gold, targeting the Ilesha Schist Belt in Osun State and prospective ground in Kwara, Oyo and — as our footprint matures — the more stable gold-producing districts of the Middle Belt such as Niger and Kogi States.

Product Line 03

Associated Minerals

Tantalite, columbite, cassiterite and gemstone by-products that commonly co-occur within the same pegmatite systems as lithium and gold, developed opportunistically as a secondary revenue stream alongside our primary targets.
