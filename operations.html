Our Strategic Roadmap

Modola's growth is structured in three deliberate phases, moving from our Lagos base into progressively more remote — and carefully vetted — mineral belts.

Growth Roadmap

Three phases, one deliberate pipeline

01
Years 1–2·Foundation & South-West Entry

Foundation & South-West Entry

Target Area: Osun, Oyo, Ekiti, Kwara

Exploration licensing, resource verification, small-scale gold and lithium (spodumene/lepidolite) operations near the Ilesha belt and South-West pegmatite fields.

02
Years 2–4·Middle-Belt Expansion

Middle-Belt Expansion

Target Area: Nasarawa, Kogi, Niger

Mining lease acquisition in Nigeria's established "Lithium Hub" and stable Middle-Belt gold districts; scale-up toward mechanized, mid-size operations.

03
Years 4+·Selective Northern Entry

Selective Northern Entry

Target Area: Vetted, low-risk LGAs in the North

Opportunistic entry only where security assessments, community relations and logistics support safe, sustainable operations.

Site-Selection Discipline

Four gates before Modola commits capital

Every prospective location is screened against four gates before Modola commits capital. Locations that fail any gate are deferred, regardless of geological attractiveness.

Gate 01

Verified mineral occurrence

Gate 02

A clean and available mineral title

Gate 03

A credible security and community-relations assessment

Gate 04

Realistic logistics for haulage and site access

Why Partner With Modola

Built for investors, communities and regulators alike

For Investors & Off-take Partners

  • Early-mover access to a phased, de-risked pipeline of South-West and Middle-Belt lithium and gold assets.
  • Nigerian-owned operator with local knowledge of licensing, land access and community engagement.
  • Alignment with Federal Government local-value-addition policy — positioning for processed, not raw, mineral supply.

For Host Communities

  • Formal engagement, local employment priority, and structured community development commitments.
  • A safety- and environment-first operator, rather than unregulated artisanal extraction.

For Government & Regulators

  • A fully compliant, tax-paying, cadastre-registered operator supporting Nigeria's solid minerals diversification agenda.
