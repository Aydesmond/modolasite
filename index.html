Who We Are

A Nigerian solid minerals company built for the long term

Modola Mining Company Limited is a Nigerian solid minerals company headquartered in Lagos, established to explore, develop and responsibly operate mineral assets across Nigeria's most prospective geological terrains. The company was founded to participate in Nigeria's accelerating minerals renaissance, with an initial strategic focus on lithium and gold, and a broader interest in the associated pegmatite and precious-metal minerals that typically occur alongside them, including tantalite, columbite, cassiterite and gemstone by-products.

Our approach is deliberately phased and risk-managed. We are building Modola from a Lagos base outward: beginning with exploration and small-scale operations in the South-West of Nigeria, and progressing over the medium to long term toward mining leases in the Middle Belt and selected, relatively stable Northern states.

What We Do

Three complementary mineral product lines

Reflecting the mineral associations most common in Nigeria's South-West and Middle Belt pegmatite and schist belt provinces.

Product Line 01

Lithium

Hard-rock spodumene and lepidolite ore, targeting the high-grade pegmatite fields of the South-West (Oyo, Ekiti, Kwara) and the Middle Belt "Lithium Hub" states of Nasarawa and Kogi.

Product Line 02

Gold

Alluvial and primary gold, targeting the Ilesha Schist Belt in Osun State and prospective ground in Kwara, Oyo and the more stable gold-producing districts of the Middle Belt.

Product Line 03

Associated Minerals

Tantalite, columbite, cassiterite and gemstone by-products that commonly co-occur within the same pegmatite systems as lithium and gold, developed opportunistically as a secondary revenue stream.
