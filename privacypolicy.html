PRIVACY POLICY

RC: 7169111

Last Updated: [August 18, 2026]

www.modolamining.com

Modola Mining Company Limited (“Modola,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits www.modolamining.com or contacts us through it. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the rights you have over it, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

1. What We Collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you submit: full name, company (optional), email address, phone number, subject and message. We do not collect any information you don't directly provide to us through the form.

2. Why We Collect It

We use this information solely to:

  • • Respond to your enquiry, whether you are an investor, off-take partner, host-community member, regulator, or another stakeholder;
  • • Maintain a record of correspondence for our own business purposes.

We do not use contact-form data for marketing, and we do not sell, rent or trade it to third parties.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the basis of consent — by submitting the form, you consent to us using your information for the purpose stated above — and, where applicable, our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

4. How Long We Keep It

We retain contact-form submissions only for as long as reasonably necessary to address your enquiry and maintain business records, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

5. Who Can See It

Submitted information is accessible only to authorised Modola personnel who need it to respond to your enquiry. We do not share it with third parties except where required by Nigerian law or regulation.

6. Your Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • • Know what personal data we hold about you;
  • • Request a copy of it;
  • • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • • Request deletion of your data, subject to any legal retention requirements;
  • • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@modolamining.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal data we hold against loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date on the cover of this document will reflect the most recent revision.
